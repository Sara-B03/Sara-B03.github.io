UX/UI Design Showcase of CustomsGPT

This project was a competition entry for Zayed University and Dubai Customs, showcasing an AI-powered customs declaration system that streamlines trade document analysis, detects compliance risks, and enhances efficiency through real-time insights and an intuitive UX/UI design. This entery won third-place on February 2025.

Log in/Sign Up Page

A clean and intuitive UX/UI concept for a login and signup interface. The design focuses on simplicity and accessibility, providing users with a seamless entry point to their accounts. Also includes a password reset feature. It balances security with usability by offering clear input fields and guided interactions that ensures a smooth onboarding experience.

CustomsGPT Chatbot Interface

Chatbot Interface page showcasing real-time analysis of customs declarations. The design is very minimal and basic to insure ease of use. includes an input prompt text field and a document/photo upload feature.

Side Menu of CustomsGPT

Side menu of CustomsGPT available on every page after logging in. Can lead users to the chatbot interface, insight dashboard, history of previously uploaded documents ad analysis, or a settings page.

Customs Insights Dashboard

A UX/UI concept showcasing real-time analysis of customs declarations. The design highlights compliance risks, anomalies, and potential fraud through data visualization, actionable insights, and user-friendly workflows.

CustomsGPT Chatbot Uploading Content

A conversational AI interface for customs document analysis. The chatbot enables customs officers to upload invoices, request anomaly checks, and receive instant compliance feedback in a simple, intuitive chat-based experience.

CustomsGPT Chatbot Analysis of Content

Example of a response from conversational AI CustomsGPT and the provided document analysis. Highlights all possible analomies and detects suspcious enteries for customs officer to review.
